# Business Context: Roshani Enterprises

## Executive Summary
Roshani Enterprises is a premier luxury real estate development and bespoke civil construction firm catering to ultra-high-net-worth individuals (UHNWIs), private family offices, and institutional investors across India and key global markets (Mumbai, Goa, Alibaug, Dubai, London).

## Dual Core Competencies

### 1. Bespoke Architectural Construction (EPC)
- Monolithic structural engineering with Grade M60 low-carbon concrete.
- Zone-4 seismic resilience and post-tensioned foundation engineering.
- Artisanal European masonry: Belgian limestone, hand-hewn travertine, and custom bronze thermally isolated fenestration.
- Sub-26 dB acoustic floating slab assemblies.
- Sustainable geothermal micro-climates and IGBC Platinum / LEED-standard design.

### 2. Prime Real Estate Brokerage & Advisory
- Exclusive off-market acquisitions for private family offices.
- Curated portfolio of coastal compounds, alpine ridge retreats, and urban sky penthouses.
- Complete regulatory transparency, RERA compliance, and NDA confidentiality.

## Brand Positioning
- **Aesthetic**: Restrained architectural permanence, monolithic luxury, warm stone, and deep obsidian bronze.
- **Values**: Structural integrity, direct partner oversight, discretion, and generational permanence.
- **Accreditations**: Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors (RICS), Indian Green Building Council (IGBC), Master Builders Guild, ISO 9001:2015.
