# Project Specification: Roshani Enterprises

## Brand Identity & Positioning
- **Entity**: Roshani Enterprises
- **Industry**: Luxury Real Estate Development & Bespoke Construction
- **Positioning**: Architectural permanence, master-planned private sanctuaries, and turnkey construction engineering for discerning private clients, family offices, and institutional real estate investors.
- **Brand Attributes**: Restrained elegance, structural integrity, artisanal materiality, architectural rigor, and discreet private advisory.

## Target Audience & Needs
1. **Ultra-High-Net-Worth Individuals (UHNWIs) & Private Clients**: Seeking custom-built private estates, modern architectural villas, and discreet off-market residential acquisitions.
2. **Family Offices & Sovereign Investors**: Demanding turnkey project delivery, institutional-grade structural engineering, strict timeline adherence, and asset appreciation.
3. **Architectural Connoisseurs**: Prioritizing bespoke materials (hand-hewn limestone, monolithic poured concrete, acoustic glass curtains, sustainable geothermal systems).

## Core Offerings
- **Bespoke Residential Construction**: Ground-up architectural villas, cliffside cantilevers, and private family compounds built to ISO 9001 and green building standards.
- **Prime Real Estate Brokerage**: Curated portfolio of prime waterfront estates, alpine chalets, and urban sky residences.
- **Master Planning & Civil Engineering**: Land feasibility, zoning, seismic engineering, and environmental stewardship.
- **Turnkey Private Advisory**: End-to-end concierge from architectural blueprint to interior curation and key handover.

## Visual Design System & Materiality
- **Color Palette**: Warm limestone & alabaster paper (`#FBF9F5`, `#F3EFE8`, `#EDE6DC`), deep obsidian-bronze ink (`#121316`, `#1A1C20`, `#272A30`), warm muted stone (`#60646D`, `#888E99`), champagne brass accent (`#C5A880`, `#9E7D52`).
- **Typography**: Display Roman Serif (`Playfair Display`, optical tracking, sentence case headings) + Clean Humanist Grotesk (`Plus Jakarta Sans`, 400/500/600).
- **Surface Language**: Solid architectural planes, crisp 1px hairline dividers (`#E4DED5`), subtle elevation through lightness, zero tacky AI slop (no generic glass blur, no pulsing status pings, no purple gradients).
- **Interactions**: 6-state coverage (default, hover, active, focus, disabled, loading), crisp 150ms-250ms cubic-bezier composite transitions, high-contrast `:focus-visible` rings, reduced-motion compliance.
